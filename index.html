About Alamo Automation

Alamo Automation Agency exists for one reason: to help San Antonio service businesses stop drowning in DMs, missed calls, and forgotten follow‑ups. We build simple, done‑for‑you automation systems that handle customer replies, booking, reminders, reviews, and lead follow‑up—so you can focus on the work you actually enjoy.

We speak plain language, not tech jargon. You tell us what’s driving you crazy, and we design a system that quietly fixes it in the background.

Services

We work with local service businesses that live on their phones and inboxes:

Customer response automation

Instant replies to hours, pricing, location, booking, and FAQs so you never miss a message.

Appointment automation

Hands‑free booking, confirmations, and reminders—perfect for barbers, nail techs, med spas, and more.

Lead follow‑up automation

We automatically follow up with leads from your website, DMs, or ads until they respond.

Review generation automation

After each visit or job, your customers get a simple message asking for a review.

Invoice & quote automation

Send quotes and invoices automatically after a request or completed job.
